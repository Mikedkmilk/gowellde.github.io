Choosing the right welding process is critical for production efficiency. Here is a breakdown of how our machines support different workflows.

## MIG/MAG (GMAW)

**Best for:** High-volume production, thick materials, long welds.

**Pros:** Fastest travel speed, continuous wire feed, easy to learn. Our MIG-500 series is built for continuous heavy-duty cycles in structural steel plants.

## Stick (SMAW/MMA)

**Best for:** Outdoor construction, maintenance repair, rusty metal.

**Pros:** No gas cylinder needed, works in wind. The ARC-400/630 series is the workhorse for infrastructure projects.

## TIG (GTAW)

**Best for:** Precision work, thin stainless steel, aluminum, aesthetic welds.

**Pros:** Highest quality welds, no spatter. Requires more skill but delivers clean results for high-end products.

> **Recommendation:** For most general steel fabrication factories, MIG/MAG offers the best balance of speed and cost.

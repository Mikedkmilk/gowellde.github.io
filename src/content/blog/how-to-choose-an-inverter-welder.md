A practical guide for industrial buyers to select the right inverter welding machine based on duty cycle, power supply, process type and application.

## Key selection criteria

- **Process:** MMA for general fabrication, MIG/MAG for productivity, TIG for precision.
- **Duty cycle:** For industrial use, target ≥60% at rated current.
- **Input power:** 1P 220V for portable units; 3P 380V for heavy-duty lines.
- **Current range:** Match thickness and wire size, e.g., 400–500A for heavy sections.
- **Environment:** Prefer robust cooling and dust protection in harsh workshops.

## Recommended models

MMA: ARC-315A/400A for industrial repair; ARC-500/630 for heavy fabrication. MIG/MAG: MIG/MMA-400/500 for dual-voltage versatility; 500CI/630B for production lines.

## Cost of ownership

Consider spare parts availability, board aging test, IGBT grade, and after-sales response time to reduce downtime risk.

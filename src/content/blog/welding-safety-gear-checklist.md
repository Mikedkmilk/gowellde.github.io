Safety is the #1 priority in any welding shop. Proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) prevents burns, eye damage, and long-term health issues.

## 1. Auto-darkening helmet

Essential for protecting eyes from UV/IR radiation. Look for a fast switching speed (1/25,000s) and large viewing area.

## 2. Welding jacket

Leather or flame-resistant cotton jackets protect skin from sparks and UV burns. Don't weld in synthetic clothes that can melt!

## 3. Respirator / fume extraction

Welding fumes (especially from stainless steel or galvanized metal) are hazardous. Use a P100 respirator or portable fume extractor.

## 4. Proper gloves

Thick leather gauntlets for Stick/MIG to block heat; thin kidskin gloves for TIG to maintain dexterity.

Stay safe and weld smart.

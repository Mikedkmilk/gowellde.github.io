Aluminum is lightweight and corrosion-resistant but tricky to weld due to its oxide layer and high thermal conductivity.

## AC TIG: the precision choice

Alternating Current (AC) TIG is the gold standard for aluminum. The positive half-cycle cleans the oxide layer, while the negative half-cycle penetrates the base metal.

- **Pros:** Beautiful, stack-of-dimes weld appearance; precise heat control for thin gauges.
- **Best for:** Aerospace parts, bicycle frames, automotive piping.

## Pulse MIG: the speed choice

Modern Pulse MIG machines can weld aluminum much faster than TIG. The pulsed current prevents burn-through and reduces spatter.

- **Pros:** 3-4x faster travel speed; easier for operators to learn.
- **Best for:** Shipyards, trailer manufacturing, heavy structural aluminum.

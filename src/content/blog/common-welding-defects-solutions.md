Even the best machines can produce defects if settings or technique are off. Here is how to fix the most common issues.

## 1. Porosity (wormholes)

**Cause:** Lack of gas coverage, wind, or dirty base metal.

**Fix:** Check gas flow (15-20 CFH), set up wind screens, and clean oil/rust from the metal.

## 2. Undercut

**Cause:** Voltage too high, travel speed too fast, or wrong torch angle.

**Fix:** Lower voltage, slow down, and point the arc more at the joint wall.

## 3. Lack of fusion (cold lap)

**Cause:** Amperage too low or travel speed too slow (weld pool gets ahead of arc).

**Fix:** Increase amperage and move the torch slightly faster to keep the arc on the leading edge.

## 4. Excessive spatter

**Cause:** Wrong polarity, wire feed too fast, or long arc length.

**Fix:** Check polarity (DCEP for solid wire), adjust inductance if available.

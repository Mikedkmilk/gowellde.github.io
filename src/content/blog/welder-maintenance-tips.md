Preventive maintenance is cheaper than repairs. Follow these simple steps to keep your GOWELLDE inverters in top condition.

## Daily checks

- Check all cable connections for tightness. Loose connections cause overheating.
- Inspect the cooling fan. Ensure it's running when the machine is on.
- For MIG welders: check the contact tip and nozzle for spatter buildup. Clean or replace as needed.

## Monthly maintenance

- **Blow out dust:** disconnect power, open the cover, and use dry compressed air to remove metal dust from the circuit boards. This is crucial for inverter longevity.
- Check gas hoses for leaks using soapy water.
- Inspect power cords for cuts or damage.

## Storage

When not in use, cover the machine to protect it from moisture and dust. Store in a dry, ventilated area.
